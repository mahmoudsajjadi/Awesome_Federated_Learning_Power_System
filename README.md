# Awesome Federated & Multi-Agent Learning in Power Systems

Welcome to the **Awesome Federated & Multi-Agent Learning in Power Systems** repository! This curated collection serves as a gateway to research papers, benchmark repositories, and resources at the intersection of federated learning, decentralized multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL), and smart power grids.

## Featured Research Papers

1. **[Sustainable Smart Grid Resilience and Renewable Integration Using Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/18/20/10574)**
   - **Authors:** Seyed Mahmoud Sajjadi Mohammadabadi, Sayed Mohammad Sajjadi
   - **TL;DR:** Introduces a two-tier decentralized multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) framework integrating feeder reconfiguration, Q-learning-based restoration, coordinated battery energy storage dispatch, and communication-efficient training (ComDML) on the IEEE 33-bus and 179-bus Oberrhein networks. Restores 55.1% isolated load across all 496 double-fault pairs with **72.7% fewer feasibility evaluations** and increases PV hosting capacity by **+51%**.
   - **Venue:** *MDPI Sustainability*, Vol. 18, Issue 20, 10574 (2026)
   - **Links:** [[Official MDPI Open Access](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/18/20/10574)] • [[DOI: 10.3390/su182010574](https://doi.org/10.3390/su182010574)] • [[Code & Benchmarks](https://github.com/mahmoudsajjadi/Smart-Grid-Resilience-MARL)] • [[Interactive Page & BibTeX](https://mahmoudsajjadi.github.io/papers/sustainable-smart-grid-resilience-marl.html)]

2. **[Generative Artificial Intelligence for Distributed Learning to Enhance Smart Grid Communication](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijin.2024.09.002)**
   - **Authors:** Seyed Mahmoud Sajjadi Mohammadabadi, Mahmoudreza Entezami, Aidin Karimi Moghaddam, Mansour Orangian, Shayan Nejadshamsi
   - **Venue:** *International Journal of Intelligent Networks*, Vol. 5, pp. 267–274 (2024)
   - **Links:** [[DOI: 10.1016/j.ijin.2024.09.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijin.2024.09.002)] • [[Interactive Page & BibTeX](https://mahmoudsajjadi.github.io/papers/generative-ai-distributed-learning-smart-grid.html)]

3. **[Feature Collusion Attack on PMU Data-Driven Event Classification](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISGT59692.2024.10454123)**
   - **Authors:** Amir Ghasemkhani, Rutuja Sanjeev Haridas, Seyed Mahmoud Sajjadi Mohammadabadi, Lei Yang
   - **Venue:** *IEEE PES Innovative Smart Grid Technologies (ISGT)* (2024)
   - **Links:** [[IEEE Xplore](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISGT59692.2024.10454123)] • [[Interactive Page & BibTeX](https://mahmoudsajjadi.github.io/papers/feature-collusion-attack-pmu-classification.html)]

4. **[Towards Distributed Learning of PMU Data: A Federated Learning-based Event Classification Approach](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10252920)**
   - **Authors:** Seyed Mahmoud Sajjadi Mohammadabadi, Yunchuan Liu, Abraham Canafe, Lei Yang
   - **TL;DR:** Proposes a privacy-preserving federated learning framework for Phasor Measurement Unit (PMU) event classification across 3,877 grid events and 23 PMUs, achieving 88–90% accuracy on non-IID utility data.
   - **Venue:** *IEEE Power & Energy Society General Meeting (PESGM)* (2023)
   - **Links:** [[IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10252920)] • [[Interactive Page & BibTeX](https://mahmoudsajjadi.github.io/papers/pmu-data-federated-learning-event-classification.html)]
